**Verification categories:** (A) named author's actual writing or published transcript readable with a passage locator; (B) ancient testimony in translation (often partisan, not the subject's autograph); (C) catalog/publisher record or published article abstract, no full argument-page verification; (D) secondary author's specific source reference, original not read. A secondary report must never be upgraded into category A by repetition. A claim that an author argued something is separate from whether the argument is historically true.
