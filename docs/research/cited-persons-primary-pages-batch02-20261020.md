## DO NOT CHANGE / source gate

- **No public biographical promotion or counts in this batch.** At the time of reading, canonical `main` was **194 DOCUMENTED, 171 UNRESOLVED (365 total)**. The separate open draft #212 is not yet canon.
- **Mackie:** preserve the distinction between rejecting objective value-properties and rejecting everyday morality; arguments about God and ethics are his contested positions, not demonstrated facts.
- **Macrina:** do not imply Gregory's speeches reproduce verbatim historical teaching; do not substitute later East/West theological generalizations for evidence about Macrina.
- **Maitzen:** never cite the **2006 demographics** paper as the source of his **2009 moral-obligation** objection; include both works if mentioning both.
- **Maimonides:** no claim he invented the *number* 613, which is already found in earlier rabbinic discussion; the methodological classification is what he contributed. Flag “the list never sorts itself” as an **inference**, not a quote.
- **Malamat:** no firsthand p. 135 and multiple incompatible secondhand formulations; keep `UNRESOLVED`.
