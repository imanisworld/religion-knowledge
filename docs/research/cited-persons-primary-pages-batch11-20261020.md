## 111. Socrates Scholasticus

**Original main claim (unmodified):** ⟨UNRESOLVED⟩ **Socrates Scholasticus** — 5th-century church historian; wrote from the pro-Nicene side. One of the two chief surviving accounts of Nicaea. **Flag:** He was a participant in the winning side's tradition, not a modern neutral historian. The entry currently has no directly linked evidence sufficient to support its full wording. Retain it only as an audit lead, not as an established fact. `UNRESOLVED 12 AUG 2026 — DIRECT SOURCE OR PAGE REQUIRED`

**Verdict:** CORRECT—late pro-Nicene historian, NOT participant in 325.

**Source and checked locator:** Firsthand translated author text: Socrates, *Ecclesiastical History* I.1, I.8–9, narrates Constantine's council, draws on Eusebius and synodal document, and admits dependence on documents and informants. In II.1 Socrates says he initially followed Rufinus and later corrected the chronology using Athanasius. https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/26011.htm ; https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/26012.htm

**Counterevidence and remaining gap:** He lived/wrote during the fifth century, **a century after Nicaea**, so 'participant in the winning side's tradition' only means later affiliation, never eyewitness participation. His own self-description asserts a truth-seeking method but does not guarantee neutrality. Compare narratives with Eusebius, Athanasius and non-Nicene witnesses.

**Safe replacement wording — not yet published:** Fifth-century ecclesiastical historian Socrates Scholasticus retrospectively recounts Nicaea in *Church History* I.8–9 from a broadly pro-Nicene standpoint, citing earlier documents and authors; he was not an eyewitness.

## Critical corrections and source decisions

1. **Preston Sprinkle**: The source self-identifies *Erasing Hell* as coauthored with **Francis Chan**; its central defense was of *irreversible judgment*, and **everlasting conscious torment was a cautious lean**, explicitly acknowledging annihilation, not an unqualified defense. Author's 2015 retrospective cites pp. **85–87**. Preserve time-specific development through 2016.
2. **Mark Preston Stone**: His 2022 original journal abstract identifies **at least 21 interpretations and ongoing disagreement**. The current 'emerging consensus' claim about orientation is **not supported** by the inspected source. This nuance matters for public discussion of Leviticus and sexuality.
3. **Eleonore Stump**: OUP says she uses narrative to construct an **extended Thomistic theodicy**, not 'narrative instead of theodicy'.
4. **Socrates and Sozomen**: Both are **later fifth-century writers**, not witnesses physically at the 325 council; documentary material must be checked for textual independence and partisan framing.
5. **Symmachus**: *neanis* variant appears in later testimonies and modern philological discussion; 'he deliberately revised the Septuagint as anti-Christian correction' goes beyond preserved evidence. Irenaeus III.21.1 names **Aquila/Theodotion**, not Symmachus.
6. **Stringfellow**: 1841 pamphlet explicitly conflates **Canaan** with **Africans descended from Ham** in racist proslavery biblical apologetics. Its claims must remain subject of research, not an authority on morality or biblical truth.
7. **Tacitus**: Latin and translation for *Annals* 15.44 checked; writer is hostile but **does not disclose his underlying informant**, so hostility alone cannot prove independent information chain.
8. **Streeter**: Original 1924 chapters VII/IX support Markan priority and a four-document hypothesis; latter remains a model, not physical documentation of hypothetical Q/M/L sources.
