## 96. Sarah of the Desert (Amma Sarah)

**Current source claim (unmodified):** ⟨UNRESOLVED⟩ **Sarah of the Desert (Amma Sarah)** — Desert Mother; one of three named women in the *Apophthegmata Patrum*. The entry currently has no directly linked evidence sufficient to support its full wording. Retain it only as an audit lead, not as an established fact. `UNRESOLVED 12 AUG 2026 — DIRECT SOURCE OR PAGE REQUIRED`

**Research verdict:** HOLDS for survival of attributed sayings; chronology/biography indirect.

**Evidence, precise locator, and source-access level:** Greek-English parallel edition, *Apophthegmata Patrum*, alphabetic Sarah **1–10**, directly contains apophthegms attributed to Amma Sarah; Sarah **1–2** recount a 13-year struggle against a sexual-temptation demon; **4** features interlocutors minimizing her because she is a woman; **5** her emphasis on purity of heart rather than praise. Text is **a later collected tradition**, not a fourth-century autograph. See https://www.ldysinger.com/%40texts/0400_apophth/greek_alph/05_rho-omegai.htm and historical annotated edition https://voicesofthefathers.org/library/sayings-of-the-desert-fathers-alphabetical/sarah

**Counterevidence and source gates:** One of three named *ammas* of the Greek alphabetical collection (Sarah, Syncletica, Theodora); don't imply only three female persons appear in all desert traditions or that a vision of a demon is an independently verified empirical event. The corpus reports sayings, not a securely dated firsthand biography.

**Proposed public-safe wording (not published):** 'The alphabetical *Sayings of the Desert Fathers* preserves a dossier of sayings attributed to Amma Sarah, one of its named desert mothers. The material is a later literary witness to early ascetic teaching and gendered authority, not a preserved Sarah autograph.'
