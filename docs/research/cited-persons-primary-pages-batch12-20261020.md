## 127. Thecla

**Original main attribution (unmodified):** ⟨UNRESOLVED⟩ **Thecla** — Literary figure in the apocryphal *Acts of Paul and Thecla* (2nd century). **Audit verdict:** HOLDS as Tertullian's hostile report; collapse the assumption that this is proven authorship of all *Acts of Paul and Thecla*.

**Evidence with access level and locator:** **Direct primary witness** Tertullian, *On Baptism* **17**, explicitly names Thecla, attributes pseudonymous writings under Paul's name to a presbyter in Asia, and reports a confession that it was written 'from love of Paul'; Tertullian rejects its use to justify women's teaching/baptizing. https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0321.htm and A. ## 134. Thucydides

**Original main attribution (unmodified):** ⟨UNRESOLVED⟩ **Thucydides** — Greek historian; *History of the Peloponnesian War* 1.22.1. States he kept as close as possible to the *general sense of what was actually said* — a claim to fidelity of gist, not a license to invent speeches wholesale. Retain it only as an audit lead, not as an established fact. `UNRESOLVED 12 AUG 2026 — DIRECT SOURCE OR PAGE REQUIRED`

**Audit verdict:** HOLDS in two original Greek-aligned translations but qualified against opposite overreading.

**Evidence with access level and locator:** Original **Greek and two separate English translations** of *History of the Peloponnesian War* **1.22.1**, directly read from Perseus: Crawley (1914) https://beta.perseus.tufts.edu/urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0003.tlg001.perseus-eng6:1.22.1/ and C. F. Smith (1919–1923) https://beta.perseus.tufts.edu/urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0003.tlg001.1st1K-eng1:1.22.1/ . **Counterevidence / missing original or contextual gate:** Both reconstructions and fidelity are explicit, so treating the passage as support for **verbatim transcripts** or **complete fabrication** is overstated. The Greek `ta deonta` and `xympases gnomes` are debated in classical scholarship; a blanket claim 'both conservatives and critics commonly misuse' needs individual source examples. The passage does **not automatically explain Acts** authorial method.

**Safer future public wording, not implemented:** 'In 1.22.1, Thucydides says that when exact speech wording was difficult to recover, he composed speeches appropriate to each occasion while aiming to retain the overall sense of what had actually been said. Scholars debate the degree of reconstruction permitted by that formulation.'
