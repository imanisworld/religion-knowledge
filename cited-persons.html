Purpose. Who each cited person is and where they are coming from. The goal is not to discredit anyone but to know what lens shaped their conclusions before using them as authorities. A slavery defender can be an excellent primary source for what proslavery Christians argued; they become a bad choice if presented as a neutral authority on whether slavery was morally acceptable.
