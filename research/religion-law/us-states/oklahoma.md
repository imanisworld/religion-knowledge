## Constitutional provisions

### Article I, §2 — Religious liberty and plural marriage

Oklahoma guarantees toleration of religious sentiment, bars molestation of persons or property because of religious worship, and bars religious tests for civil or political rights. The same section expressly prohibits polygamous or plural marriages.

- **Legal effect:** State religious-liberty and no-religious-test protection combined in the same provision with an explicit prohibition on plural marriage.
- **Classification:** D for the liberty/test protections. The anti-polygamy language is explicit law touching a practice historically associated with religion; a B/C historical-causation classification requires separate history and is not assigned solely from the text.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

### Article II, §5 — Public money or property for sectarian purposes

Oklahoma bars public money or property from being used directly or indirectly for the use, benefit, or support of sects, churches, denominations, systems of religion, religious teachers or dignitaries, or sectarian institutions as such.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force, with application subject to controlling federal constitutional doctrine.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Education and public funding

### Article I, §5 — Public schools free from sectarian control

Oklahoma requires a public-school system open to all children and free from sectarian control.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.
