## Constitutional provisions

### Article I, §4 — Guaranty of religious liberty

Idaho guarantees the exercise and enjoyment of religious faith and worship, bars denial of civil or political rights because of religious opinion, bars compelled support of ministry or worship and legal denominational preference, and expressly states that liberty of conscience does not excuse specified unlawful conduct. The same provision expressly prohibits bigamy and polygamy.

- **Legal effect:** State religious-liberty and noncompulsion protection with explicit limits on conduct.
- **Classification:** D for the religious-liberty rule.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

The placement of anti-polygamy language inside the religious-liberty clause is textually explicit. A historical-causation classification concerning religion and anti-polygamy policy requires separate convention-era research.

## Education and public funding

### Article IX, §5 — Sectarian appropriations prohibited

Idaho bars appropriations and public payments in aid of churches, sectarian or religious societies, sectarian or religious purposes, or educational institutions controlled by religious denominations, subject to the section's stated health-facilities financing exception.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force, with application subject to controlling federal constitutional doctrine.
- **Confidence:** High.

### Article IX, §6 — Religious tests and teaching in public schools

Idaho bars religious tests or qualifications for admission to public educational institutions as teacher or student, bars required participation in religious services, and bars sectarian or religious tenets or doctrines from being taught in public schools.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Historical or unenforceable rules

The Idaho Attorney General has described Article IX, §5 as a "Blaine Amendment." That descriptor does not, by itself, establish a single anti-Catholic or other historical motive for Idaho's provision; causation remains open for separate evidence.
