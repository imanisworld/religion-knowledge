## Constitutional provisions

### Article II, §11 — Freedom of religion

New Mexico protects worship according to conscience, bars denial of civil or political rights because of religious opinion or mode of worship, bars compelled attendance at worship or compelled support of a religious sect or denomination, and bars legal preference for a religious denomination or mode of worship.

- **Legal effect:** State constitutional protection of conscience, equal civil/political status, noncompulsion, and religious nonpreference.
- **Classification:** D — religion-limiting rule.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Education and public funding

### Article XII, §3 — Control of education and use of educational funds

New Mexico requires constitutional educational institutions to remain under state control and bars specified educational funds from being used for support of sectarian, denominational, or private schools, colleges, or universities.

- **Legal effect:** State constitutional restriction on use of educational funds for sectarian, denominational, and private institutions.
- **Classification:** D for the religion-specific portions.
- **Status:** In force; application is subject to controlling federal constitutional doctrine and state case law.
- **Confidence:** High.

Because the clause covers private institutions generally as well as sectarian/denominational institutions, its full scope should not be described as exclusively religion-targeted.
