## Constitutional provisions

### Article I, §3 — Religion

Iowa bars laws respecting an establishment of religion or prohibiting free exercise. It also bars compelled attendance at worship and compelled tithes, taxes, or other payments for building or repairing places of worship or maintaining a minister or ministry.

- **Legal effect:** State constitutional establishment, free-exercise, and noncompulsion protection.
- **Classification:** D — religion-limiting rule.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

### Article I, §4 — Religious test; civil rights; witnesses

Iowa bars religious tests for office or public trust and bars deprivation of rights, privileges, capacities, duties, or witness competence because of religious opinions.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.
