## Constitutional provisions

### Article 1, §18 — Religious liberty and civic capacity

Wyoming protects free exercise and enjoyment of religious profession and worship without discrimination or preference. Religious belief may not make a person incompetent to hold office or serve as a witness or juror. The section also states that liberty of conscience does not justify licentious acts or practices inconsistent with the peace or safety of the state.

- **Classification:** D — religion-limiting rule.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

### Article 1, §19 — Public money for religious institutions

Wyoming provides that no money of the state shall ever be given or appropriated to a sectarian or religious society or institution.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force, subject to controlling federal constitutional doctrine in its application.
- **Confidence:** High.

### Article 7, §§8 and 12 — Religious control, tests, and instruction in education

Article 7, §8 restricts public-school funds from private schools or institutions of learning controlled by a church, sectarian organization, or religious denomination. Article 7, §12 bars sectarian instruction, qualifications, or tests in state-controlled schools and institutions, bars required attendance at religious services, and bars teaching or favoring sectarian tenets or doctrines.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force, subject to controlling federal constitutional doctrine where applicable.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Religious accommodation and liberty

### W.S. §§9-29-101 through 9-29-104 — Wyoming Religious Freedom Restoration Act

Wyoming's Religious Freedom Restoration Act provides that state action may not substantially burden a person's exercise of religion, including through a rule of general applicability, unless the government demonstrates that the burden is essential to further a compelling governmental interest and is the least restrictive means of furthering that interest.

The Act supplies a claim or defense and authorizes appropriate relief, including declaratory and injunctive relief under its terms.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Effective date:** July 1, 2025.
- **Confidence:** High.

The 2025 session law originally enacted the provisions with different chapter numbering; the current consolidated Wyoming Statutes place the Act at §§9-29-101 through 9-29-104. The current codification controls this profile.
