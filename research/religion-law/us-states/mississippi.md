## Constitutional provisions

### Section 18 — Freedom of religion

Mississippi bars religious tests for office and legal preference for a religious sect or mode of worship, and protects free enjoyment of religious sentiments and modes of worship subject to public-morals, peace, and safety limits.

The same section states that the protected rights shall not be construed to exclude the Holy Bible from use in any public school.

- **Legal effect:** State constitutional no-religious-test, nonpreference, and free-exercise protections; the text also contains an explicit Bible-use proviso for public schools.
- **Classification:** D for the liberty/no-test provisions; A for the explicit sacred-text proviso.
- **Status:** In force in the Secretary of State's official constitutional text.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Research caution

The Constitution permits/non-excludes use of the Bible in public schools; that is not the same as a constitutional mandate that public schools conduct devotional Bible instruction.
