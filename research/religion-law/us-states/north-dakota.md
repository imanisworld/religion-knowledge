## Constitutional provisions

### Article VIII, §1 — Public schools free from sectarian control

North Dakota requires a public-school system open to all children and free from sectarian control.

- **Legal effect:** State constitutional requirement that the public-school system remain free from sectarian control.
- **Classification:** D — religion-limiting rule.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

### Article VIII, §5 — Public-school funds and sectarian schools

The Constitution states that money raised for support of the public schools may not be appropriated to or used for support of any sectarian school.

- **Legal effect:** State constitutional restriction on use of public-school support funds for sectarian schools.
- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force, but application is subject to controlling federal Free Exercise doctrine.
- **Confidence:** High.
