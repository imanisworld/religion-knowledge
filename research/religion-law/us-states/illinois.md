## Constitutional provisions

### Article I, §3 — Religious freedom

Illinois guarantees free exercise and enjoyment of religious profession and worship without discrimination, bars denial of civil or political rights because of religious opinion, bars compelled attendance or support of ministry or worship, and bars legal preference for a denomination or mode of worship.

- **Classification:** D — religion-limiting rule.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Education and public funding

### Article X, §3 — Public funds for sectarian purposes forbidden

Illinois bars appropriations or public payments in aid of a church or sectarian purpose or to support educational institutions controlled by a church or sectarian denomination, and bars public grants or donations to a church or for a sectarian purpose.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

The Illinois Legislative Research Unit states that this language was taken verbatim from the 1870 Constitution. Historical motive beyond that textual lineage remains a separate research question.

## Religious accommodation and liberty

### Illinois Religious Freedom Restoration Act — 775 ILCS 35/15

Illinois law provides that government may not substantially burden a person's exercise of religion, including through a generally applicable rule, unless the burden furthers a compelling governmental interest by the least restrictive means. The Act also provides a claim or defense and judicial relief.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.
