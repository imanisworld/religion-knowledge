## Constitutional provisions

### Article II, §4 — Religious freedom

Colorado guarantees the free exercise and enjoyment of religious profession and worship without discrimination, bars denial of civil or political rights because of religious opinion, bars compelled attendance or support of a ministry or place of worship, and bars legal preference for a religious denomination or mode of worship.

- **Legal effect:** State constitutional protection for religious exercise, conscience, noncompulsion, and denominational neutrality.
- **Classification:** D — religion-limiting rule.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Education and public funding

### Article IX, §7 — Aid to private schools, churches, and sectarian purposes

Colorado bars the state and specified public bodies from appropriating or paying public money in aid of a church or sectarian society, for a sectarian purpose, or to support educational institutions controlled by a church or sectarian denomination. It also bars grants or donations of land, money, or other personal property to a church or for a sectarian purpose.

- **Legal effect:** State constitutional no-aid restriction.
- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force; application remains subject to controlling federal constitutional doctrine.
- **Confidence:** High.
