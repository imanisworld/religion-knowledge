## Constitutional provisions

### Article I, §4 — Religious freedom

Nebraska protects worship according to conscience, bars compelled attendance or support of worship, bars legal preference for religious societies and interference with conscience, bars religious tests for office and witness disqualification for religious belief, and directs the Legislature to protect religious denominations in peaceful worship.

- **Legal effect:** State constitutional conscience, noncompulsion, nonpreference, and no-religious-test protections.
- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Education and public funding

### Article VII, §11 — Public funds, sectarian instruction, religious tests

Nebraska generally bars appropriations to schools or institutions of learning not owned or exclusively controlled by the state or a political subdivision, subject to stated exceptions for specified services. It requires public schools to be free of sectarian instruction and bars state acceptance of money or property for sectarian purposes subject to the federal-grant proviso. The section also addresses religious tests for teachers and students.

- **Legal effect:** Broad public-school/public-funding restrictions with explicit sectarian-instruction and religious-test rules.
- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

The Legislature's official annotations include cases applying the provision to Bible reading, hymns, prayer, and aid to religious schools.
