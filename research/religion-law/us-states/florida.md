## Constitutional provisions

### Article I, §3 — Religious freedom

Florida bars laws respecting an establishment of religion or prohibiting or penalizing free exercise. The section states that religious freedom does not justify practices inconsistent with public morals, peace, or safety. It also bars state or local public revenue from being taken directly or indirectly in aid of a church, sect, religious denomination, or sectarian institution.

- **Legal effect:** State establishment/free-exercise protection plus a constitutional no-aid rule.
- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Religious accommodation and liberty

### Florida Religious Freedom Restoration Act of 1998 — Fla. Stat. ch. 761

Section 761.03 bars government from substantially burdening a person's exercise of religion, including through a generally applicable rule, unless the burden furthers a compelling governmental interest by the least restrictive means. It authorizes the affected person to assert a violation as a claim or defense and obtain appropriate relief.

Section 761.05 states that the Act applies broadly to state law unless a later law expressly excludes it and also states that the Act does not alter the establishment provisions of the Florida or U.S. Constitutions.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force in the 2026 Florida Statutes.
- **Confidence:** High.
