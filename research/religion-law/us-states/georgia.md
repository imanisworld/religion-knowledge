## Constitutional provisions

### Article I, §I, ¶III — Freedom of conscience

Georgia's current Constitution states that each person has a natural and inalienable right to worship God according to conscience and that no human authority should control or interfere with that right.

- **Legal effect:** State constitutional protection of religious conscience.
- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

### Article I, §I, ¶IV — Religious opinions; freedom of religion

Georgia bars molestation of a person or property and exclusion from public office or trust because of religious opinions, while stating that religious freedom does not excuse licentious acts or practices inconsistent with state peace and safety.

- **Legal effect:** Protects persons from specified state disabilities based on religious opinion.
- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Education and public funding

### Article I, §II, ¶VII — Separation of church and state

Georgia bars money from being taken from the public treasury, directly or indirectly, in aid of a church, sect, cult, religious denomination, or sectarian institution.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Research caution

The Constitution expressly invokes God and protects religious liberty, while also restricting treasury aid to religious institutions. These provisions address different legal functions and are recorded separately.
