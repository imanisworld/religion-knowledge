## Constitutional provisions

### Article I, §4 — Liberty of conscience

Nevada guarantees free exercise and enjoyment of religious profession and worship without discrimination or preference and bars witness incompetence based on religious belief, subject to peace-and-safety limits on conduct.

- **Legal effect:** State constitutional free-exercise, nonpreference, and witness protections.
- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Education and public funding

### Article XI, §9 — Sectarian instruction

Nevada bars sectarian instruction in schools or universities established under the Constitution.

### Article XI, §10 — Public money for sectarian purposes

Nevada bars state, county, and municipal public funds from being used for sectarian purposes.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force, subject to controlling federal constitutional doctrine.
- **Confidence:** High.
