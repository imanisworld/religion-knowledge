# Missouri — Religion & Law Profile

**Research status:** First-pass constitutional baseline verified; statutory and historical expansion pending  
**Last verified:** 2026-09-26  
**Confidence:** High for cited constitutional text

## Constitutional provisions

### Article I, §5 — Religious freedom

Missouri protects conscience and worship, bars religious disqualification from public office, testimony, or jury service, bars an official religion and coercion into prayer or religious activity, and contains detailed protections for voluntary prayer and religious expression in public settings and schools.

- **Legal effect:** State constitutional conscience, non-establishment, no-religious-disqualification, and religious-expression protections.
- **Classification:** D — religion-limiting / religious-exercise rule.
- **Status:** In force; current text reflects a 2012 amendment.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Education and public funding

### Article I, §7 — Public treasury and religious aid

Missouri bars public-treasury money from being taken directly or indirectly in aid of a church, sect, denomination, clergy, or teacher of religion as such, and bars religious preference or discrimination.

### Article IX, §8 — Public aid for religious purposes and institutions

Missouri separately bars state and local public appropriations or payments in aid of religious creeds, churches, sectarian purposes, or educational institutions controlled by religious bodies, and bars specified public grants/donations for religious purposes.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force, subject to controlling federal constitutional doctrine.
- **Confidence:** High.
