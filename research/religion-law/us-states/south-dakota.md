# South Dakota — Religion & Law Profile

## Constitutional provisions

### Article VI, §3 — Freedom of religion; support of religion prohibited

South Dakota protects worship according to conscience, bars denial of civil or political rights because of religious opinions, bars compelled support of a ministry or place of worship, bars legal preference for a religious establishment or mode of worship, and bars state money or property from being given or appropriated for the benefit of a sectarian or religious society or institution.

- **Classification:** D — religion-limiting rule.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

The official Constitution records that a 2023 proposed amendment affecting this section was rejected on November 5, 2024; the current published text remains operative.

## Education and public funding

Article VI, §3 supplies the first-pass state constitutional funding rule by barring state money or property for the benefit of sectarian or religious societies or institutions. Program-specific education funding questions remain pending.

## Religious accommodation and liberty

### SDCL 1-1A-4 — Government authority; exercise of religion

South Dakota bars state and local government from substantially burdening a person's exercise of religion unless the burden is essential to a compelling governmental interest and is the least restrictive means. It also bars treating religious conduct more restrictively than comparable secular conduct and provides judicial remedies.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force; enacted in 2021.
- **Confidence:** High.

### SDCL 22-19B-4 and 22-19B-5 — Interference with religious practice

Section 22-19B-4 makes intentionally preventing another person, by threat or violence, from performing a lawful act enjoined or recommended by that person's religion a Class 6 felony. Section 22-19B-5 makes intentionally attempting by threats or violence to compel another person to adopt, practice, or profess a particular form of religious belief a Class 1 misdemeanor.

- **Classification:** D — rules protecting religious practice and limiting religious coercion.
- **Status:** In force; §22-19B-4 reflects a 2026 amendment in the current code.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Historical or unenforceable rules

### Chapter 22-26 — Sabbath Breaking

South Dakota's codified-law index retains Chapter 22-26 under the title “Sabbath Breaking” and marks the chapter repealed by Session Laws 1976, chapter 158, §26-1.

- **Classification:** A — explicit religion-linked legal category in the historical code.
- **Status:** Repealed.
- **Confidence:** High for repeal status and chapter title.
- **Causation note:** The explicit Sabbath label establishes a religion connection in the legal text; this first pass does not separately assign a broader historical-causation claim.
