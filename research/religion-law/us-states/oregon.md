## Constitutional provisions

### Article I, §§2–4 — Worship, conscience, and religious tests

Oregon protects worship according to conscience, bars laws controlling free exercise of religious opinions or interfering with rights of conscience, and bars religious tests for offices of trust or profit.

- **Classification:** D — religion-limiting rule.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

### Article I, §5 — Treasury appropriations for religion

Oregon bars money from being drawn from the Treasury for the benefit of religious or theological institutions and bars appropriations for payment of religious services in either house of the Legislative Assembly.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force, subject to controlling federal constitutional doctrine.
- **Confidence:** High.

### Article I, §6 — Religious tests for witnesses or jurors

Oregon bars witness or juror incompetence based on religious opinions and bars questioning a witness in court about religious belief for purposes of affecting testimonial weight.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Religious accommodation and liberty

Oregon statutes recognize religion as a protected characteristic in employment, housing, and public accommodations and contain specified exceptions for bona fide religious institutions.

- **Primary example:** ORS 659A.006.
- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Current religion-linked statutes

- ORS 659A.006 — anti-discrimination policy including religion, with specified religious-institution exception.
- ORS 350.640 — contracts for nonsectarian/nonreligious educational services and prohibition on use of disbursed funds for religious purposes.
