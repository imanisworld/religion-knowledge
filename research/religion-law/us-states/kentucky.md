## Constitutional provisions

### Section 5 — Right of religious freedom

Kentucky bars preference by law to a religious sect, society, denomination, creed, mode of worship, or ecclesiastical polity. It bars compelled attendance or contribution to religious worship or ministry, protects against compelled education contrary to conscience, bars civil-rights changes based on religious belief or disbelief, and protects rights of conscience.

- **Legal effect:** State constitutional conscience, noncompulsion, nonpreference, and religious-equality protection.
- **Classification:** D — religion-limiting rule.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Education and public funding

### Section 189 — School money not to be used for church, sectarian, or denominational school

Kentucky's Constitution states that no portion of funds or taxes raised or levied for educational purposes shall be appropriated to or used by, or in aid of, a church, sectarian, or denominational school.

- **Legal effect:** State constitutional restriction on specified educational funds benefiting religious schools.
- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force, subject to controlling federal constitutional doctrine and current Kentucky case law.
- **Confidence:** High.
