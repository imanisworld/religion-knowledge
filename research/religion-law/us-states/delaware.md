## Constitutional provisions

### Article I, §1 — Freedom of religion

Delaware's Constitution uses explicitly religious language, including a statement concerning public worship of Almighty God, but its operative protections bar compelled attendance or support of worship or ministry, bar magistrates from controlling rights of conscience in religious worship, and bar legal preference among religious societies, denominations, or modes of worship.

- **Legal effect:** State constitutional protection of conscience, free religious worship, noncompulsion, and nonpreference.
- **Classification:** D for the operative legal protections.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

The explicit theological wording is recorded rather than silently omitted. This first pass does not characterize the prefatory “duty” language as a separately enforceable legal obligation.

### Article I, §2 — Religious test for office not required

No religious test may be required as a qualification for state office or public trust.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.
