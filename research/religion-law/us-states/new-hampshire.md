## Constitutional provisions

### Part I, Article 5 — Religious Freedom Recognized

New Hampshire's Constitution protects an individual's right to worship according to conscience and protects persons and property from restraint based on religious profession, sentiments, or persuasion, subject to public-peace limits.

- **Legal effect:** State constitutional free-exercise/conscience protection.
- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

New Hampshire official statutory and Secretary of State materials continue to identify Part I, Article 5 as the state constitutional free-exercise provision.

## Religious accommodation and liberty

### RSA 546-C — Protection of Religious Liberty during states of emergency

Current RSA 546-C defines religious organizations and religious services and, during a state of emergency, requires state government to permit religious organizations to operate and engage in religious services to the same or greater extent as other organizations or businesses providing essential services. Neutral health, safety, and occupancy requirements remain possible, while substantial burdens on religious services require a compelling governmental interest and least restrictive means.

- **Legal effect:** Religion-specific emergency-operation protection with claim/defense and relief provisions.
- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force; chapter source identifies 2021 enactment.
- **Confidence:** High.
