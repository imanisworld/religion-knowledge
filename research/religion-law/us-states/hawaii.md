## Constitutional provisions

### Article I, §4 — Freedom of religion

Hawaii bars laws respecting an establishment of religion or prohibiting the free exercise of religion.

- **Legal effect:** State constitutional establishment and free-exercise protection.
- **Classification:** D — religion-limiting rule.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Education and public funding

### Article X, §1 — Public education

Hawaii requires a statewide public-school system free from sectarian control and prohibits discrimination in public educational institutions because of religion, among other protected characteristics. It also bars public funds from being appropriated for the support or benefit of sectarian **or nonsectarian** private educational institutions, subject to the section's specified special-purpose-revenue-bond exceptions.

- **Legal effect:** Public-school nonsectarian-control rule, nondiscrimination rule, and a broad private-school funding restriction with express exceptions.
- **Classification:** D for the religion-related portions.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

Because the funding restriction covers nonsectarian as well as sectarian private institutions, it should not be described simply as a prohibition aimed only at religion.
