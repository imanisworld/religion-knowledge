# U.S. 50-State Religion & Law Research Index

This directory is a research scaffold, not a completed legal database.

## Status rules
- **Baseline record present:** at least one structured state record exists; the state is not necessarily complete.
- **Anchor priority — research pending:** prioritized for the first detailed state batch.
- **Scaffold only — research pending:** no state-level legal conclusion should be inferred.

Maryland already has a substantive structured baseline at `../records/us-md-maryland.json`. All other state files remain research-pending until separately verified.

## Promotion rule
A state profile is not verified until substantive entries have primary legal authority, separate explanatory/causation sourcing where needed, current-status verification, and a recorded verification date.
