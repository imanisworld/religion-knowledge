## Constitutional provisions

### Article I, §16 — Freedom of conscience

Minnesota protects worship according to conscience, bars compelled attendance or support of worship or religious ministry, bars governmental interference with conscience and legal preference for a religious establishment or mode of worship, and bars treasury money for the benefit of religious societies or religious/theological seminaries.

- **Legal effect:** State constitutional conscience, noncompulsion, nonpreference, and no-aid protections.
- **Classification:** D — religion-limiting rule.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

### Article I, §17 — Religious tests prohibited

Minnesota bars religious tests as qualifications for public office or voting and bars witness incompetence based on religious opinion.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Education and public funding

### Article XIII, §2 — Sectarian-school aid

Minnesota bars public money or property from being appropriated or used to support schools in which the distinctive doctrines, creeds, or tenets of a particular Christian or other religious sect are promulgated or taught.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force, subject to controlling federal constitutional doctrine.
- **Confidence:** High.
