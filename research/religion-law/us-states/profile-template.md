# U.S. State Profile Template

## Required status
- Research status: scaffold / partial / verified / needs re-review
- Last verified: YYYY-MM-DD once verified
- Confidence: high / medium / low / contested

## Required sections
1. Constitutional provisions
2. Education and public funding
3. Religious accommodation and liberty
4. Current religion-linked statutes
5. Historical or unenforceable rules
6. Landmark cases
7. Evidence and sources

## Evidence rule
Every completed legal entry requires exact primary legal authority, a neutral statement of legal effect, separate historical-causation evidence where claimed, competing causes or limitations where material, current status, verification date, and confidence.

Do not convert a scaffold placeholder into a legal conclusion without source verification.
