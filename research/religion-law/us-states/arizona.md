## Constitutional provisions

### Article II, §12 — Liberty of conscience

Arizona Constitution art. II, §12 bars public money or property from being used for religious worship, exercise, instruction, or support of a religious establishment. It also bars religious qualifications for public office or employment and protects witnesses and jurors from religious disqualification.

- **Legal effect:** No-establishment/public-aid restrictions plus a no-religious-test rule.
- **Classification:** D — religion-limiting rule.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

### Article XI, §7 — Public education

Article XI, §7 bars sectarian instruction in state-established schools and educational institutions and bars religious or political tests as a condition of admission or participation as teacher, student, or pupil.

- **Legal effect:** Restricts sectarian instruction and religious testing in public education.
- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Religious accommodation and liberty

### A.R.S. §41-1493.01 — Free exercise of religion protected

Arizona law states that free exercise of religion is a fundamental right, including when a government burden results from a facially neutral, generally applicable rule. Government may substantially burden religious exercise only when the burden advances a compelling governmental interest through the least restrictive means. The statute provides a claim or defense and relief against government.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.

### A.R.S. §41-1493.04 — Licenses and public appointments

Arizona law also restricts government from denying, revoking, or suspending professional or occupational credentials for specified religion-based decisions or expression, subject to statutory and constitutional limitations. It separately bars denial of certain public appointments based on a person's exercise of religion.

- **Classification:** D.
- **Status:** In force.
- **Confidence:** High.
