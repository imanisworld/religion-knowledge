# Montana — Religion & Law Profile

**Research status:** Anchor school-aid issue verified; broader state profile pending
**Last verified:** 2026-09-26

## Education and public funding

Montana Constitution art. X, §6 prohibits certain public appropriations or payments for sectarian purposes or to institutions controlled by a church, sect, or denomination.

- **Primary authority:** Montana Constitution art. X, §6.
- **Controlling case:** *Espinoza v. Montana Department of Revenue*, 591 U.S. 464 (2020).
- **Legal effect of decision:** Montana could not apply its no-aid provision to exclude otherwise eligible religious schools from the scholarship program at issue solely because of religious status.
- **Classification:** State no-aid text = D as a religion-limiting funding rule; federal Free Exercise constraint = D.
- **Status:** Constitutional text remains, but its application is limited by federal Free Exercise doctrine.
- **Confidence:** High for text and case holding.

## Historical-causation caution

The origins of Montana's no-aid provision should be researched separately before assigning anti-Catholic, secularist, public-school, or other causal labels.
