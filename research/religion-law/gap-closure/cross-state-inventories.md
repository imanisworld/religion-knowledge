## Source hierarchy

Prefer, in order:

1. Current state constitution/statutory code or official court rule.
2. Session law and official legislative history where historical development matters.
3. Controlling state/federal cases.
4. Official agency guidance.
5. Reputable secondary legal scholarship for context and source discovery.

Commercial summaries may identify leads but do not verify a row.
