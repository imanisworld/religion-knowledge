## Evidence standard

- **Verified passage / specific authority:** named source, passage or statutory section independently accessible. Only what that passage proves is recorded.
- **Corroborated secondary history:** explanatory guide, legal scholarship or later judicial retrospective analysis, attributed to that author and date.
- **Unverified passage:** inaccessible legislative transcript, historical testimony, or original scan. Keep explicit uncertainty.
- **No inference from missing documents:** an unlocated religious lobby or hearing statement does not prove it never existed.
- **Political officeholder affiliation:** report published, time-limited self-description or membership without making a current-membership claim from historical status alone.
