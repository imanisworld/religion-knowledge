## Evidentiary decision

- All ten URLs reached relevant materials in the source check. **Link accessibility does not equal claim-level evidentiary adequacy.**
- The specific **Adventist 1844 sanctuary interpretation** is verified directly in official General Conference belief 24. Catholic typology and rejection of millenarianism are likewise anchored to official catechism sections. The OCA overview includes some **specific** cited Old Testament examples.
- **Most of the 141 short Christian-perspective paragraphs have only approach-level citations**, not a passage by the named interpreter that treats the exact biblical verse. They must remain explicitly **illustrative Christian readings**, awaiting direct church commentary, named author's page or recognized edition. A linked source defining futurism does not prove a particular Christian denomination endorses *this exact verse-by-verse future scenario*.
- At the site, keep direct original historical record, later Christian theological reception, and independently corroborated fulfillment separately labeled.
